Relevant Information:
Different types of grains were used in this research, taking into account the features such as form, shape, type, and structure by the market situation. 

Attribute Information:
1.) Area (A): The area of a grain zone and the number of pixels within its boundaries.
2.) Perimeter (P): Grain circumference is defined as the length of its border.
3.) Major axis length (L): The distance between the ends of the longest line that can be drawn from a grain.
4.) Minor axis length (l): The longest line that can be drawn from the grain while standing perpendicular to the main axis.
5.) Aspect ratio (K): Defines the relationship between L and l.
6.) Eccentricity (Ec): Eccentricity of the ellipse having the same moments as the region.
7.) Convex area (C): Number of pixels in the smallest convex polygon that can contain the area of a grain seed.
8.) Equivalent diameter (Ed): The diameter of a circle having the same area as a grain seed area.
9.) Extent (Ex): The ratio of the pixels in the bounding box to the grain area.
10.)Solidity (S): Also known as convexity. The ratio of the pixels in the convex shell to those found in grains.
11.)Roundness (R): Calculated with the following formula: (4piA)/(P^2)
12.)Compactness (CO): Measures the roundness of an object: Ed/L
13.)ShapeFactor1 (SF1)
14.)ShapeFactor2 (SF2)
15.)ShapeFactor3 (SF3)
16.)ShapeFactor4 (SF4)
